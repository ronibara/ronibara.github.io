Physiology

Building a foundation in health science.

Coming from sports betting, I wanted to understand the behind-the-scenes (health science) before forming any opinions of what's on the surface (user experience). Claude helped me get there: pulling Locker articles on physiology, explaining the concepts from first principles, and connecting them to what the app was actually doing. The cards below are the three areas that grounded everything else.

01 Heart Rate & HRV

Heart rate is WHOOP's only raw input — everything else is derived from it. HRV, the fluctuation between beats, is the nervous system's report card: high variability signals restoration, low variability signals stress or depletion.

02 Sleep Architecture

Sleep cycles through four stages roughly every 90 minutes. Deep sleep physically repairs the body; REM mentally restores it. The circadian rhythm demands consistency — irregular schedules degrade quality even when total hours look fine.

03 Physiological Age

Your body has two ages: chronological and physiological. Sleep, cardiovascular fitness, strength, and daily movement all determine how fast your biology ages — and WHOOP is one of the first consumer products to surface that number daily.

Core Features

Getting to know the product firsthand.

I bought a WHOOP 5.0 and used the Claude curriculum alongside it — connecting what I was feeling day to day to what the app was surfacing.

01 Recovery Score

A morning readiness score (0–100%, color-coded red/yellow/green) driven primarily by HRV. It's forward-looking — telling you what your body can handle today. The average member scores ~58%, making yellow the emotional default state of the product.

02 Strain & Sleep

Strain measures cardiovascular and muscular exertion on a 0–21 logarithmic scale — deeply personal, and only meaningful read against Recovery. Sleep Performance tracks four inputs: sufficiency, consistency, efficiency, and stress.

03 Journal & WHOOP Age

The Journal bridges data and behavior — logging 300+ habits to surface personal correlations with Recovery. WHOOP Age, a six-month rolling physiological estimate, shifts the product's value from daily performance coaching toward long-term longevity management.

Users & Landscape

Orienting to WHOOP's strategy and where I can contribute.

Once I had a foundational sense of the product, Claude helped me map the broader landscape: who WHOOP is built for, how it sits against Oura, Garmin, and Apple Watch, and where it's headed as it scales into longevity and AI.

01 Three User Archetypes

WHOOP's base has evolved from elite athletes to a broader 25–45 health-conscious demographic. Three archetypes now define the product: Performance Optimizers, Health Improvers, and Longevity Seekers — each mapped to a distinct membership tier.

02 Competitive Positioning

WHOOP beats Oura on training load coaching, Garmin on recovery depth, and Apple Watch on focus. Its no-screen, subscription-only design is a deliberate filter — attracting committed users and raising the bar for casual adoption.

03 The AI Roadmap

WHOOP is building toward a health operating system — connecting wrist biometrics, Journal data, and blood biomarkers through four AI pillars: memory, adaptive coaching, voice integration, and Advanced Labs. 600+ new hires in 2026 signal the start of its most ambitious phase yet.
