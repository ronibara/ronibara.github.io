About Me



Dear WHOOP Hiring Team,

My interest in WHOOP started with the people around me: friends sporting WHOOP straps, Oura rings, and Garmin watches. Over time, my own life caught up. I bought a WHOOP 5.0 and dove deep into The Locker – the physiology first, then the part I couldn't stop thinking about: the habit loop of checking recovery each morning, the psychology of trusting a single number enough to change your behavior, and the gap between where the Journal sits today and where adaptive coaching could take it. That's the research problem I want to work on.

At FanDuel, I've spent the last two and a half years leading research across new product verticals, primarily prediction markets. My job has been translating complex, unfamiliar financial trading concepts into something a casual sports fan could trust and act on, defining where FanDuel Predicts builds confidence and where it loses people. That same challenge sits at the core of WHOOP, whether you're designing for performance optimizers, health improvers, or longevity seekers.

My toolkit started almost entirely qualitative – interviews, usability sessions, think-alouds – and expanded naturally into mixed methods: MaxDiff surveys, diary studies, and newer tools like AI moderation. Beyond methods, I've built an AI-first research workflow from the ground up, using purpose-built prompts to sharpen study design with research rigor, pressure-test recommendations wearing multiple hats, and frame insights in a corporate strategy lens. Altogether, I believe this combination maps well to this role.

I'm genuinely excited about Boston, this industry, and this moment for WHOOP as a company. More than contributing to the product, I see this as a place where I can grow my career doing work that fulfills me.

Sincerely,
Rohan Barad
